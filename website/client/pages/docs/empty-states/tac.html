Empty states

w-empty-state (alias w-empty) communicates that there is nothing to show. It supports a leading icon or image, a large headline, title/text, an action-text button, color/bg-color, and justify alignment. The title renders as a real heading element, <h2> by default; set heading-level (1 to 6) to fit the page, or none for a plain paragraph. A slotted title that is already a heading keeps its own level.
