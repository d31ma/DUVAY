Send status

w-send-status shows the state of one message or list row that was written on the device and still has to reach the server: waiting, sending, failed or sent. Every state shows an icon and words, never colour alone. The element never takes focus; buttons in its actions slot keep their normal tab order. The state is called "waiting", not "queued", because a mail app may already use "Queued" for its server's mail queue.

An empty state shows nothing, and sent without written-at shows nothing, so a row that was never offline stays quiet. With reduced motion the spinner is replaced by a still arrow icon.

Actions

Put the app's buttons in the actions slot. They wrap under the words on a narrow screen.

What a screen reader hears

The words are read with their row; the element is not a live region. When a row changes to failed, the element counts it through the page's shared polite announcer (WLiveAnnouncer, key w-send-status, 1 second). Twenty rows failing at once make one announcement: "20 not sent. Needs your attention." waiting, sending and sent are never announced, and a row that is first shown already failed is not announced. Set announce="off" on a row that must not count.

Translated text

Every text has an English default and an attribute to replace it. {time} is filled with the written time in the language of the closest lang.

hour-cycle="h23" or "h12" chooses 24 or 12 hours for the written time; without it the language decides. The announcement has plural forms: failed-announce-text-one, -other (and -zero, -two, -few, -many for languages that need them), chosen with Intl.PluralRules for the total count, for example failed-announce-text-one="{count} item not sent." and failed-announce-text-other="{count} items not sent.". A missing form falls back to -other, then to failed-announce-text.

"Needs your attention" list

A list of the items the server refused is a pattern of existing parts, not a new element:

  • w-list with aria-label="Needs your attention".
  • One w-list-item item-role="none" per refused item, with an id. title is the first words of the item, subtitle is where it belongs (for example the conversation). A static row shows its default content under the subtitle. Add wrap so a long title is shown whole instead of cut, and no-prepend-space so the text starts at the row padding (these rows have no icon).
  • In that content, a w-send-status state="failed" with the reason, and the action buttons in its actions slot.
  • Each button says what it acts on: the visible word ("Retry") plus aria-describedby="<row id>-title". A list item with an id gives its title the id <row id>-title.
  • When the list is empty, a w-empty-state with the title "Nothing needs your attention". Its title renders as an <h2>; set heading-level to fit the page's headings.

Try it: Retry sends the item again and it leaves the list; Discard removes it; "Fail again" refuses every item again at once.

Under each OS tab the failed icon and the buttons follow that platform: a filled "!" badge on iOS, macOS, Android and Windows, symbolic outline icons on Linux, and the buttons at the row's trailing edge on Windows and Linux. Colours, sizes and text stay the same on every skin.
